# Experience design: Vanta

Vanta is a personal portfolio first, and an expressive instrument studio second. Neither should become a generic game menu or a technology demo that speaks louder than the work.

## StoryStack, adapted to an artist's studio

| Layer | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| Fantasy | Enter a working artistic practice and explore its connections through sound, space, and evidence. |
| Action | Explore a room or work; influence an instrument's parameters; discover the audible, visual, or documented consequence. |
| Economy | Attention, listening, connections between pieces, and verifiable sources. No artificial XP or points. |
| World | Five channels of one practice, expressed through the portfolio, atlas, and walkable studio. |
| Story | A visitor finds a work, experiments with it, and follows a thread to another discipline. |

## A first minute worth having

1. **Explore:** enter the studio, select a room, or take the text index when WebGL is unavailable.
2. **Influence:** use an instrument with a real parameter that changes what is heard or shown. Make the gesture legible; don't require a cinematic tour first.
3. **Discover:** name the response and offer a source, artifact, or neighboring work worth following.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Sound starts silent and never autoplays. Keep the existing audio cap, reduced-motion, mobile, keyboard, and fallback behavior.
- A sound visualization reacts to the instrument's actual parameters, not to decorative randomness passed off as evidence.
- A simulation is labelled as simulation. A publication is not called verified merely because it is linked.
- The text index and original portfolio remain complete alternatives to the 3D studio.
- Preserve the project's sharp-edged, restrained visual system and canonical content in `data/practice.js`. No generic AI-themed graphics.

## Acceptance questions

Can an unfamiliar visitor choose a first action, get feedback, and reach a checkable source without instructions? Can a phone visitor do the same? Is the work still recognizably Vanta when the scene is off?
