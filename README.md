# Vanta

**An interactive studio, research atlas, and portfolio by Christopher Woodyard.**

Start in the [studio](https://mitpress.vercel.app/studio) and play, or open the [research atlas](https://mitpress.vercel.app/research) and follow the questions behind the work. The [portfolio](https://mitpress.vercel.app/) brings the instruments, simulations, paintings, and poems together.

I make music as [Indigo People](https://chriswoodyard.bandcamp.com/) and run [Vers3Dynamics](https://vers3dynamics.com/), an independent, open-source research lab. I keep coming back to sound, perception, and the ways an instrument can help us notice something we missed.

You can also [play the Listening Room](https://vanta-studio-resonance.ciao-chris.chatgpt.site/studio/), a prototype of Vanta’s hands-on instrument studio.

If something catches your interest, I'd love to hear about it. You can find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/topherchris420) or [send me a note](mailto:christopher@vers3dynamics.com).

*Five notes. One chord.*

**The Music of the Primes** ([open the instrument](https://mitpress.vercel.app/riemann)): hear the oscillatory terms of Riemann’s explicit formula and compare a finite reconstruction against the prime-power staircase. The November 2026 proof announcement motivates the experiment; it is not a validation of the proof.

**The Geometry of Computation** ([open the instrument](https://mitpress.vercel.app/nine-fourths)): edit integer matrices, compare exact classical and Strassen work counts, and listen to the resulting matrix. The nine-fourths exponent is a theoretical reference, not a browser algorithm.

The studio invites you to **explore, influence, and discover**: enter a room, try an instrument, observe what changes, then follow the source of the work. Sound begins only when you choose it, and the non-3D index remains a complete route through the practice. [Experience design](docs/EXPERIENCE_DESIGN.md).
